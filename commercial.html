Selected collaborations / branded environments / production systems

Commercial collaborations

Selected work across immersive design, projection mapping, branded experience, music video production, AR systems, and spatial storytelling for clients ranging from global brands to publishers, labels, and broadcast studios.

Approach

Commercial work is treated as an extension of the larger practice: cinematic image systems, spatial thinking, emerging technology, and atmosphere translated into campaigns, live events, installations, and interactive tools.

The through-line is not branding for its own sake, but building memorable environments and experiences that carry a distinct visual logic.

Migos Hologram Activation — Proto

Holographic activation / live presence / multi-city campaign

In collaboration with Proto, this campaign brought Migos to life as full-scale holograms across multiple cities simultaneously, extending the idea of performance presence into distributed physical space.

Under Armour — Coaches Armoury

Pixel-streamed Unreal platform / athlete development ecosystem

Built as a digital performance and learning environment, Coaches Armoury used Unreal Engine and pixel streaming to create an interactive platform for athlete development, training content, and engagement.

Bose AR Adventure

Designed as an audio-driven, location-aware narrative layer for Bose AR wearables, this work explored how spatial sound and movement could carry story without relying on screens.
