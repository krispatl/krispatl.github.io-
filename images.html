I build portals—some shimmer in public parks, others glow behind museum walls. My work in installation art and projection mapping is a practice of spatial storytelling: conjuring speculative architectures, translating data into emotion, and letting light inhabit material.

Below are fragments of that journey—rituals of presence, encoded transmissions, temporal anomalies...

Selected Works

Liquid Light

A 30-meter projection-mapped waterfall cascading down the Woodruff Park water wall. Commissioned by Dashboard and powered by networked 40K lumen projectors, this piece became a living monument to perseverance.

AJC Article

Stargazer

An immersive, hybrid installation bridging virtual and physical worlds. Presented at Ars Electronica, Stargazer connected global VR participants in Mozilla Hubs with local audiences at a Barcelona dome projection site. Real-time interaction between users influenced the dome’s projections, exploring themes of distance, presence, and communication.

Collaborating Artists: Kris Pilcher, Cenk Guzelis, Mohsen Hazrati, and Espronceda Institute of Art & Culture

Fermi Paradox

A data-driven projection system drawing from Kepler’s exoplanet archive, visualizing the silence of the cosmos in computer graphics. Curated by Dashboard.

Stronger Together
