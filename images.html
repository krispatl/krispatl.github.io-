EXUVIA

Interdisciplinary performance installation · Ailin Bars + Kris Pilcher · NUBIA Labs / ESPRONCEDA

EXUVIA is an interdisciplinary performance installation directed by Ailin Bars and developed with Kris Pilcher, proposing an immersive experience where body, technology, and matter are intertwined in real time.

The work merges visual arts, digital art, contemporary dance, and live sound into a performative ecosystem in which gesture is translated into image, sound, and trace through motion sensors, digital capture, and real-time generative systems.

Presented within the framework of NUBIA Labs at ESPRONCEDA, the project explores the body as a sensitive interface capable of activating multiple perceptual layers and building a shared experience between performer, technology, and audience.

Transient Pulse

Eyedrum · solo exhibition · 2017

A study in rhythm, reflection, and pulse — a show concerned with flicker, repetition, and human-scale time.

Shown in Atlanta during a period of intense experimental output, the work used light and repetition to turn bodily tempo into visual atmosphere.

CYDONIA

A speculative Martian topography installed at Waves.GIF in Mammal Gallery, Atlanta — part ruin, part transmission.

The installation folded astronomy, conspiracy residue, and landscape fiction into an environment that felt excavated rather than simply displayed.

Project Bluebeam

A solo exhibition framed as transmissions from the Black Knight Satellite, shown at Nine Ace Gallery in Atlanta.

Working through signal, paranoia, and speculative cosmology, the project treated exhibition space as a site of intercepted messages and unstable evidence.

Vast Active Living Intelligence System

Twenty-five electrical outlets, twenty-five apparitions — a haunted grid of human-machine presence.

The work imagines infrastructure itself as sentient residue, turning familiar hardware into an uncanny network of latent life and distributed perception.
