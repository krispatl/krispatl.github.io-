Dream Collection Agency

A long-form immersive project exploring dreams as memory, source material, and shared cultural record.

Project overview

The Dream Collection Agency is a speculative installation and transmedia research project exploring the boundaries between reality, memory, and the subconscious.

Originally conceived as a performative sci-fi corporation, the DCA collects, preserves, and reactivates human dreams using a combination of volumetric capture, custom environments, and XR storytelling systems.

Housed in custom-built Dream Experience Facilities®, the agency guides participants through dream recall sessions that are translated into visual form. These sessions use multisensory cues, projection mapping, and biometric feedback to access memory states and blurred liminal moments.

The project asks: what happens when dreams are archived like data? Can the subconscious be preserved? And if so, who owns the past that never happened?
